Why do some boards have huge white regions?

White cells are open cells visited by the validated solution. With --wander-full, each initial move takes its full available length. Successive turns can spiral inward and fill a broad open region before tweaking begins. Tweaks then develop the remaining area, leaving much of that open region intact.

Before the repair, at 500×500, full-slide seed 101 already contained an all-open 273×273 square before tweaks; seed 303 contained a 487×487 square. Full-slide initial walks now stop after eight segments by default. Every tweak-generated output must also pass a quality guard: its largest open square cannot exceed one fifth of the board side (with an eight-cell allowance for small puzzles). The 18 affected saved specimens have replacements under this policy; see the repair record. The ordinary generator default uses random initial lengths.
