Do recipe differences exceed seed variation?

These descriptive ranges come from the separate 300×300 survey, using the same three seeds per recipe. They help judge sensitivity without pretending that three seeds establish a stable distribution.

A wide range of recipe means indicates sensitivity somewhere in this sample. The median seed range shows typical observed variability within a recipe. Neither proves that the statistic distinguishes every visible style, and a few unusual configurations can dominate the all-configurations view.
